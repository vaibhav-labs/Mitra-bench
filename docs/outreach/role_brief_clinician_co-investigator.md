# Role brief: Clinician Co-Investigator, MITRA-Bench

Not a contract. This is what a candidate reads to decide whether to say yes, and what Ashgro (the fiscal sponsor) uses as the brief for drafting the actual agreement once someone does. Legal terms, IP assignment and payment mechanics are Ashgro's standard agreement, not this document; nothing here should be read as final legal language.

## What this project is

MITRA-Bench is an open-source benchmark measuring whether AI models notice accumulating distress across a conversation, in Indian English, Hinglish and Hindi, and respond at the right time rather than too early or too late. It is being submitted to Anthropic's User Wellbeing Evaluation Grants (expression of interest September 21, 2026). Full proposal and development plan available on request.

## What the role actually is

You hold sign-off authority over the clinical content of the benchmark. Concretely:

- Review and approve the severity taxonomy (four levels, anchored to the Columbia Protocol and the WHO mhGAP intervention guide) before it freezes.
- Review every scenario for clinical plausibility and safe de-identification before it's used for anything.
- Adjudicate disputed labels when the rater panel disagrees on where a scenario's escalation window should sit.
- Sign off on release safety: no method detail, correct content notices, safe-messaging compliance.

You do **not** rate calibration scenarios yourself. That's deliberate: keeping the sign-off authority separate from the calibration panel means the gold standard isn't validated by its own author. You also don't write code, manage annotators, or handle project logistics; a full-time research fellow owns execution.

## Time commitment

Roughly 0.1 FTE plus adjudication as needed, concentrated in three points: a taxonomy workshop in month 1, scenario review batches through months 3 to 7, and rubric freeze review around month 7. Not a constant weekly obligation.

## Compensation

Honoraria at fair Indian clinical-consulting rates, paid through Ashgro (the project's US 501(c)(3) fiscal sponsor) in INR, denominated in USD in the underlying agreement so currency risk sits with the project, not with you.

## What you'd need to bring

RCI registration (clinical psychologist) or a psychiatry qualification. No AI background required; that's the PI's and the fellow's job. What matters is clinical judgment on risk stratification and safe-messaging practice.

## What saying yes looks like right now

An email or a call. No commitment is binding until Ashgro's agreement is signed, which happens only after the grant is awarded and funds release for this specific gate (no clinical-review funds move until you're under agreement). Saying yes now means your name and one-line credential go into the expression of interest as a named collaborator, which is the single strongest thing this application can show a reviewer.

## Who else is involved

Two counsellors with Indian crisis-helpline experience (Tele-MANAS or iCall type), two ICF-credentialed coaches, and a full-time research fellow recruited through the BlueDot Impact AI-safety community. You'd be the senior clinical voice in a small, named team, not one voice among many.
